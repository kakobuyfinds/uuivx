Shopping guides

DgoBuy finds and current product links

Use the spreadsheet entry to identify the product and color. On the seller page, check the variant name, listing availability and return window. Save the current listing link with the exact color and size, especially if several variants share one photo.

DgoBuy shoe sizing and clothing fit

For sneakers, compare the insole length in centimeters with a pair you wear. For clothing, measure a similar garment laid flat. Compare chest width, shoulder width and garment length using the seller’s measuring points. Check whether a waist measurement is flat or around the body.

DgoBuy QC photos and order checks

Match warehouse photos to the order’s size label and color. Request a close-up of outsole stitching, a zipper or a jewelry clasp when the existing angle hides it. Ask for a ruler photo if fit depends on one measurement and check the return window before requesting a replacement.

DgoBuy shipping costs by destination

Compare freight quotes for the same postal code, packed dimensions and tracking level. Check whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight. For a US, UK or EU delivery, confirm applicable taxes and import charges for the destination before comparing landed costs.

Frequently asked questions

What is a DgoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a directory of product leads, usually grouped by category. Open the current listing to confirm the seller, variant, availability and price before you decide.

How do I choose a sneaker size?

Measure an insole from a pair that fits and compare it in centimeters with the seller’s measurements for the exact model and size.

What can warehouse QC photos tell me?

Photos tied to your order can show visible condition, stitching and labels. For fit, ask for measurements. For authenticity, look for traceable seller documentation.

How do I estimate international shipping?

Get a quote for your destination using the packed weight and dimensions, then add applicable seller delivery, service fees, taxes and duties to your item price.

Is this an official DgoBuy or brand store?

This is an independent shopping guide. The category links open CAFANS, and the brand images are style references.
